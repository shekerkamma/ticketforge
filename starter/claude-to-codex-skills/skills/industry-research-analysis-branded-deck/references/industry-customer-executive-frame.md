# Industry Customer Executive Frame

Use this skill as a four-lens chain, not as a slide-export shortcut.

## Lens 1: Industry

Answer:

- What structural shifts matter in this market?
- Where is AI already landing with real operational value?
- Which lanes are crowded, early, or strategically attractive?

Capture:

- market structure
- adoption signals
- competitive pattern
- strategic implications

## Lens 2: Customer

Answer:

- Which customer segment, buyer, or named account is in scope?
- What business goal or operational pain do they actually own?
- What systems, workflows, or constraints shape adoption?

Capture:

- ICP or account
- buyer and workflow owner
- business pain
- current process
- blockers and change triggers

## Lens 3: Use Cases

Answer:

- Which use cases map to the customer's real workflow?
- Which use cases are valuable, feasible, and urgent?
- Which use cases deserve sequencing rather than parallel pursuit?

Capture:

- use-case cluster
- business job
- value logic
- feasibility/risk
- proof points

## Lens 4: Executive Action

Answer:

- What decision should the executive make?
- What should be piloted, funded, deferred, or rejected?
- What outcomes should be measured?

Capture:

- recommendation
- sequencing
- governance concerns
- KPI / decision metric

## Optional machine-readable sidecars

When this wrapper is active, prefer structured JSON sidecars for the executive layer:

- `customer-brief.json`
- `strategy-brief.json`
- `use-case-priorities.json`
- `executive-angle.json`

These should tighten the handoff between research, strategy, prioritization, and deck rendering.

## Minimum deck standard

Every strong deck should make these four questions obvious:

1. What is changing in the industry?
2. Which customer or buyer should we optimize for?
3. Which use cases matter most?
4. What should the executive team do next?
